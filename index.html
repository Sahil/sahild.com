about

Sahil Desai is an improv comedian and software engineer in Los Angeles. By day he builds iOS apps at Google. By night he runs a comedy show where the performers are drunk.

He likes systems that are well designed and scenes that are barely holding together. Both, ideally, in the same week.

He lives in Los Angeles with Montague, a mini dachshund with strong opinions about blankets.

drunk theatre

Sahil founded and runs Drunk Theatre, an improv comedy show in Los Angeles and San Francisco. The premise is simple: improvisers, alcohol, an audience. What could go wrong.

The show runs regularly in both cities, plus special engagements like UCB. In 2027, Drunk Theatre heads to the Edinburgh Fringe Festival.
